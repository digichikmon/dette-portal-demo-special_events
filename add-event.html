Behind the scenes

Only special event activities are accepted here. Wedding and real estate activities are handled on their own separate portals.

2

Time synced to client's local timezone

A client in Houston sees Houston time. A client in Dubai sees Dubai time. No manual conversion needed on either end.

3

Synced to your Google Calendar

4

Confirmation sent to client

The client receives a summary with the date, time in their local timezone, and the activity details.

In this prototype, "Add to calendar" triggers a confirmation toast only. Timezone conversion and Google Calendar sync would be wired in the real build.
